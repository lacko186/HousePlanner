# HousePlanner
Have you ever run into a situation where, during a home renovation, everything started blurring together and you didn't even know who sent which invoice—yet you still needed to submit them to the bank? By the time submission comes around, the invoices are piled high, and sorting them out after the fact would take up huge amounts of time.

HousePlanner provides the solution to this: it brings clarity to your processes, lets you track expenses, and enables you to manage everything in one single place
